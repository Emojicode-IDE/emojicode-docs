# Privacy and Analytics

This IDE and its documentation use analytics to improve the service and make it easier to use. There is no login, and we do not collect any personally identifiable information such as your name or email address.

## Analytics

We use [Google Tag Manager](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/tag-manager/) and [Google Analytics](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics/). Google Analytics uses cookies to send information such as page views to Google. For details on what information is collected and how it is handled, please see [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites?hl=en).

## What we do not send

The code you write in the editor, standard input, run results, and saved templates are stored only in your browser and are never sent to a server or to analytics. Compilation and execution also take place entirely in your browser.

## Opting out

If you install the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout?hl=en), information will no longer be sent to Google Analytics. You can also configure your browser to refuse cookies. In either case, you can continue to use the IDE as usual.
